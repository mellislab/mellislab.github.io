Yogesh is an Assistant Professor of Cell and Developmental Biology and a member of the Center for Synthetic Biology at Northwestern University. Scientifically, he has a multidisciplinary background, including undergraduate training in chemical engineering, graduate research in quantitative developmental biology, and postdoctoral experience in single-cell biology. Yogesh leads an interdisciplinary, collaborative, vibrant, and supportive research team. Outside of research, Yogesh enjoys sports, drawing and engaging in art-science projects, pumpkin spice lattes, and bantering with Ian.
