Our research spans a broad range of topics related to pathogenic viruses, gene and cell therapies, and adaptive immune responses. We integrate diverse approaches from the fields of molecular systems biology, virology, and immunology to study gene regulation, build methods for genetic engineering, characterize virus evolution, propose vaccine candidates, engineer gene therapies, and mitigate immune responses that interfere with gene and cell therapies.

We are affiliated with the [Department of Pathology and Cell Biology][1] and the [Aaron Diamond AIDS Research Center][2] at the [Columbia University Irving Medical Center][3]. 

Interested in learning more? See our [research interests][4] and [publications][5] to read about our latest work. Check out [news][6] for lab events, honors, and updates. 

We emphasize interdisciplinary collaboration and personalized mentorship, and we're always on the lookout for motivated candidates interested in [joining the group][7].
